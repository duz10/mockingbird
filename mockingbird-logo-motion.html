Mockingbird · Motion · v1.0

Logo motion

Three motion states for the Mockingbird mark. The ellipses are the living element — they expand into being, oscillate while listening, collapse into stillness. The play triangle stays still as the anchor.

§ 01 — Splash

Entry & exit

Ellipses expand vertically from their centers, staggered left to right — voice traveling toward the output. The play triangle scales in last, settling the composition. Use on app launch, login load, and any "voice is now active" moment.

§ 02 — State

Idle ↔ active

When Mockingbird is idle, the three ellipses collapse to circles — quiet, waiting. The play triangle stays as the anchor. When recording begins, the ellipses extend vertically and start oscillating: the logo becomes the waveform.

State · Idle

§ 03 — Active loop

The voice, alive

Each ellipse oscillates on its own period — 1.35s, 1.65s, 1.15s — and starts at a different phase. The mismatched timings prevent a synchronized pulse and create the irregular "this is real voice" feel. The triangle stays still; it's the steady output the energy is flowing toward.

Looping · ease-in-out · 1.15–1.65s ● Live

§ 05 — Usage

When to use each

Splash entry

App launch · Login completion · Onboarding step transitions · "Voice is now ready" moments. Once per session; not for ambient state changes.

Idle state

Listening pill at rest · Menu bar icon · Anywhere the logo appears in an interactive surface that could become active.

Active loop

While recording · Real-time transcription in progress · Wherever the user needs unambiguous "yes, audio is being captured right now" confirmation.

Static

Marketing surfaces · Documentation · App icon · Anywhere the logo represents the brand rather than a state. Static is the default — animate with intent.

Note · accessibility

All three motions respect prefers-reduced-motion. When reduced-motion is on, splash collapses to a 200ms fade and the active loop stops oscillating — the listening pill instead shows the green dot pulsing alone.
